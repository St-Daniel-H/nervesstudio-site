Delete your Rocket Ball data

For the Android game Rocket Ball by NervesStudio.

In the app

  1. Open Rocket Ball and go to the menu.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

This removes your coins, owned cars, unlocked items, campaign progress, player name, settings and ad counters from your phone. If you signed in with Google Play Games, it also replaces your cloud save with a fresh start. It can't be undone.

What we keep

Rocket Ball keeps your progress on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is also stored in your Google account (cloud save), and your highest campaign level is shown on the Google Play Games leaderboard. We don't keep this data ourselves. Our room server does not keep your matches or messages. Purchases you made are recorded by Google Play, not by us.

To remove your leaderboard entry and the rest of your Play Games data, use the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data) or your Google account settings.

Can't open the app?

Email support@nervesstudio.com from the address you use, say what you want deleted, and we will reply and confirm.

Uninstalling the game also removes all of its local data.
