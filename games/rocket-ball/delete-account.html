Delete your Rocket Ball data

For the Android game Rocket Ball by NervesStudio.

In the app

  1. Open Rocket Ball and go to the menu.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

This removes your coins, owned cars, player name, settings and ad counters from your phone and signs you out. It can't be undone.

What we keep

Rocket Ball currently stores your progress only on your phone, so deleting it there removes it. Our room server does not keep your matches or messages. Purchases you made are recorded by Google Play, not by us. You can manage them in your Google account.

Can't open the app?

Email support@nervesstudio.com from the address you use, say what you want deleted, and we will reply and confirm.

Uninstalling the game also removes all of its local data.
