Delete your Rocket Ball data

For the Android game Rocket Ball by NervesStudio. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

In the app

  1. Open Rocket Ball and tap Settings on the menu.
  2. Tap Account (top right).
  3. Tap Delete account & data under Danger zone, read the warning, type DELETE and confirm.

It can't be undone. Coins you bought are not refunded.

What is deleted

  • On your phone: your coins, owned cars and cosmetics, campaign progress, player name, settings and ad counters. If you signed in with Google Play Games, your cloud save is replaced with a fresh start.
  • On our server: your online profile (player ID, name, friend code, country, trophies, wins and losses, last IP address), your match history, your friends, friend requests and blocked list, your notifications, and the reports you made about other players.

What we may keep

  • If you were ever warned or banned, we keep a minimal record (your account ID, the warning and ban reasons and dates, and the IP address tied to a ban). Without this, deleting an account would be a way to escape a ban. We delete it when it is no longer needed to protect other players.
  • A report another player made about you may be kept for abuse prevention. It is no longer linked to your account ID, but it may still contain the name you used.
  • Your leaderboard entry and Play Games profile are held by Google, not by us. To remove them, use the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account & data) or your Google account settings.
  • Purchases you made are recorded by Google Play, not by us.

Can't open the app?

Email support@nervesstudio.com from the address you use, say what you want deleted and, if you can, your friend code (shown in the game under Profile), and we will reply and confirm.

Uninstalling the game also removes all of its local data (but not your data on our server; use the steps above or email us for that).
