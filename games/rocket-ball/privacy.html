Privacy Policy

Rocket Ball by NervesStudio. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

This policy explains what information Rocket Ball uses and why. Playing offline (campaign, against the bot, two players on one phone, same Wi-Fi) needs no account and sends nothing to us. If you sign in with Google Play Games to play online, we store some information about you on our server, described below. Contact: support@nervesstudio.com.

Information stored on your phone

The game saves your settings, your coins, the cars and cosmetics you own, how many reward ads you watched today, and your campaign progress. This is kept on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your coins, cars, unlocked items, record of purchases and campaign level is also saved to your Google account (cloud save). It is removed from your phone when you uninstall the game or use Delete account & data in the app.

Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

Signing in is optional; offline play works without it. If you sign in, Google Play Games gives the game your Play Games player ID and display name. The game uses Google Play Games to:

  • show the campaign leaderboard: your highest campaign level is sent to Google and shown to other players with your Play Games name and profile picture, according to your Play Games profile settings;
  • keep a cloud save in your Google account, so you can restore your progress on a new phone. This is stored by Google and covered by Google's Privacy Policy;
  • sign you in to our online services: the game asks Google for a one-time code, and our server uses it to confirm with Google who you are. We do not receive your Google email address or password.

Online play: what our server stores about you

To offer ranked play, friends and moderation, our server keeps a profile for each signed-in player. It contains:

  • Identifiers: your Play Games player ID, and a short friend code that other players use to find you. Players never see your Google ID or email.
  • Your player name (3 to 12 characters, unique, chosen by you; you can change it once every 24 hours). It is shown to other players: in matches, on friends lists, on leaderboards and in reports. Do not use your real name or personal details.
  • Ranked results: trophies, league, wins and losses (lifetime and per season), and a match history (who played, who won, how it ended). Match history is deleted after 90 days. Players who have not played for 30 days are hidden from the ranked top list.
  • Friends: your friend list, friend requests sent and received, and players you blocked. Friends can see whether you are online (unless you hide it in the Friends settings), your rank and trophies, and when you were last online, and can invite you to a game.
  • Reports: after a quick or ranked match you can report your opponent. A report contains the reason you pick, an optional message, the match room code, and the names involved. Only we see reports; the reported player is not told who reported them.
  • Warnings and bans: if we warn or ban a player, we record the reason, the date and, for a ban, its end date.
  • Country: the two-letter code of your country (for example MY or LB), taken from your connection when our host provides it, otherwise from the region setting of your phone. We use it only in totals, to see where our players are and where to run game servers so matches lag less. We do not store your city or an exact location.
  • Notifications: the messages shown in the in-game bell (gifts of coins from us, warnings, friend requests). We keep the newest 50 per player.
  • IP address: your server connection reveals your IP address. For signed-in players we store the IP address of your latest connection. We use it only to enforce bans and prevent abuse. When we ban a player, the ban can apply to their IP address as well as their account, and banned players are shown the reason for their ban. We do not use it for advertising, location tracking or profiles.

During a match, the server passes your player name and game messages (controls and match state) to your opponent. We do not store those game messages. Our hosting provider (Render) runs the server and our database (hosted in Frankfurt, Germany) and may keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, for a short time.

Players who are not signed in can still use quick match and room codes; the server then sees their IP address and passes the name they type to their opponent, but stores no profile.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with other companies, except the service providers named here (Google, Render) that run the features.

Same-Wi-Fi play uses your local network only; nothing goes through our server.

Why we use this information

To run the features you asked for (ranked play, friends, leaderboards, cloud save), to keep the game fair and safe (reports, warnings, bans, blocking repeat offenders), to send you messages about your own account (gifts, warnings), and to fix problems.

Ads

Rocket Ball shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch an ad for a reward) and, in the campaign, a full-screen ad every few levels, through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and similar regions, Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change that choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy (this button appears where Google requires it). See how Google uses data in ads and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

If you buy coins, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game that a purchase was made so it can give you your coins.

Children

Rocket Ball is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Your online profile is kept while you use the game. Match history is deleted after 90 days. When you delete your account, your profile, friends, match history, notifications and the reports you made are deleted. We keep a minimal record (account ID, the warning and ban reasons and dates, and the IP address tied to a ban) for players who were warned or banned, so that deleting an account cannot be used to escape a ban. Reports other players made about you may be kept for abuse prevention; they are no longer linked to your account ID but may still contain the name you used.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can delete everything the game stores, on your phone and on our server, at any time from Menu → Settings → Account → Delete account & data (you confirm by typing DELETE). If you were signed in to Google Play Games, this also replaces your cloud save with a fresh start. Your leaderboard score and Play Games profile are held by Google; you can remove your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app settings or through your Google account. See how to delete your data. You can also ask us by email to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

Security

Connections to our server use encryption (wss/HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the information described here to what the features need.

Changes

If the game starts using new kinds of information, we will update this page and the date above before it happens.
