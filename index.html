Rocket Ball

A fast 2-player football game for Android. Your car shoots balls at the big ball to score in the other team's goal.

Play your way: against a bot, a friend on the same phone, a friend over the internet with a room code, or a random player.

Collect cars: unlock new cars, each with its own speed, weight and shots.
